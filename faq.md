# Anticipating questions before they’re asked.

## Which work model do I prefer?

The work models that suit me best are hybrid or fully remote, ideally with the option to work on-site when needed. I live in Kościerzyna, approximately 50 kilometers from Gdańsk.

I am also open to regular on-site work when the nature of the role requires it, for example when working directly with hardware.

## Do I have experience with {X}?

In many cases, the honest answer will probably be "no." I have not worked professionally with RTOSes, 5G/6G standards, the telecommunications sector, or truly embedded systems.

That said, I would still encourage you to give me a chance. I am prepared to learn unfamiliar technologies from scratch and to demonstrate that my emphasis on adaptability is more than just a claim.

More broadly, low-level software engineering is exactly the kind of environment I find appealing, so moving into areas such as embedded systems or telecommunications would be a direction I would genuinely like to pursue.

## What about web development—web applications and similar technologies?

I do not tend to shy away from unfamiliar areas. At Samsung, I independently designed, built, and developed a Django-based internal inventory system from scratch for tracking the test boards used in our laboratory. More recently, I also created a personal wedding website using Django. :)

I find the fundamentals of the web genuinely fascinating, but I would rather not work exclusively as a web application developer. I see web development more as a complementary skill than as the primary field I would like to specialize in.

**But remember, I generally don't avoid technologies just because they fall outside my main area of expertise.**
